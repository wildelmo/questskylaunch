GRAVITY GARDEN

plant planets · grow orbits · defend them from the poachers

Best in the Meta Quest browser — on a Quest 3, Mixed Reality floats the garden in your real room, and invading ships tear in through your actual walls. Press the red beacon (or G) to start the invasion; the trigger becomes a ray blaster. On a flat screen: drag a planet to throw it, drag space to look, click to shoot, scroll to zoom, T trails, Space pause, C clear, 1/2 time.
