Skylaunch

You are standing in a field in the Sierra foothills at half past four in the afternoon. In a moment you will start going up, and you will not stop until the whole planet fits inside your eyes.

Trigger / A — launch, then pause and resume
Thumbstick — push forward to climb faster, pull back to slow or descend
Thumbstick press — back to normal speed
B / Y — comfort vignette on and off
Desktop preview — drag to look, space to launch, R to restart
H — hide every piece of text, here and in the world
